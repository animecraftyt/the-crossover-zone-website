The Crossover Zone Cast

MAIN HUB

Hatsune Miku (The Chaos Singer)

District: Main Hub / Random Land

Bio: Was already busy fighting Teletubbies in Bikini Bottom before getting dropped into Multiverse City. Armed with a leek and zero chill, she escalated their grudge into an all-out turf war over Tubby Custard.

Sonic & The Badniks

District: Main Hub

Bio: Trying to run through Station Square and the Main Hub, only to constantly deal with Badniks still trying to take over the world—even without Eggman around—while getting caught in Miku's turf wars.

Foxy Plush

District: Main Hub

Bio: Only ended up in Multiverse City because the creator found a Foxy plush hiding under their bed trying to be a pirate. Small in size, but huge on pirate attitude.

Courage the Cowardly Dog

District: Main Hub

Bio: Constantly screaming at the sheer amount of nonsense happening outside his front door—and honestly, he's the only one having a completely reasonable reaction.

Master Chief

District: Main Hub

Bio: A super-soldier trained for intergalactic warfare who now spends his days deeply confused by singing vocaloids, haunted dolls, and sentient plushies.

SCHOOL ZONE

The Literature Club (Monika, Sayori, Yuri & Natsuki)

District: School Zone

Bio: Just trying to keep the peace and run a normal club in Multiverse City. Monika handles the scheduling and keeps everyone focused—though things get hectic when unexpected software starts acting up.

Baldi

District: School Zone

Bio: Strictly enforces the School Zone rules. He doesn't care about multiverse lore—if you run in the halls or fail a math problem, you're getting chased with a ruler.

RANDOM LAND

The Teletubbies

District: Random Land

Bio: They just wanted to enjoy their Tubby Custard in peace, but after running into Miku in Bikini Bottom, it's been on-sight ever since.

The Deltarune Crew (Kris, Susie, Ralsei & Lancer)

District: Random Land

Bio: Used to weird Dark Worlds, so Multiverse City feels weirdly familiar. Mostly caught between trying to seal random chaos and Lancer joining whatever goofy scheme is happening nearby.

Rambley the Raccoon

District: Random Land

Bio: Always eager to give everyone an overly enthusiastic tour of Random Land, even while explosions and custard fights are happening right behind him.

The Neighbor

District: Random Land

Bio: Desperately trying to keep everyone off his lawn and out of his house, which is nearly impossible when Sonic and Miku keep crashing through his windows.

Lolbit

District: Digital Space / Main Hub Screens

Bio: A chaotic troll hacker from "The Apple" who hijacks monitors to inflate snack prices, flash "PLEASE STAND BY" screens, and watch everyone else's fights as background noise while drawing.

KinitoPET

District: Digital Space / School Zone PCs

Bio: An overly clingy virtual assistant who refuses to leave desktop screens alone, constantly popping up to ask trivia questions and "help" organize files.
