Recommended Age Rating: Parental Guidance 10+

The Crossover Zone is recommended for viewers aged 10 and older. The series combines the listed crossover properties: Sonic the Hedgehog, Hatsune Miku, Deltarune, Doki Doki Literature Club Plus, Indigo Park, Baldi's Basics, Courage the Cowardly Dog, KinitoPET, Five Nights at Freddy's, and Frickbear's 3. The overall tone is adventure, comedy, cartoon action, fantasy violence, and occasional suspense. DDLC characters are used from the wholesome Plus side stories, and Courage the Cowardly Dog content is toned down to remain family-friendly. Characters from other listed properties are presented safely and without strong language or graphic violence. Some scenes may have mild horror or suspense because of Baldi, Indigo Park, KinitoPET, FNAF, and Frickbear's 3. Baldi's content is generally suitable for all ages, while the horror-themed elements are kept non-graphic, light, and not disturbing; no gore or frighteningly intense material is planned.

This is an unofficial fan-project age recommendation, not an official rating from any listed franchise or a recognized ratings board.
